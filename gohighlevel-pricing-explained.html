GoHighLevel Pricing Explained (2026): What You'll Actually Pay

Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

GoHighLevel's pricing page shows three numbers: $97, $297, $497. Those are real, and they're flat-rate regardless of team size — genuinely one of the platform's best features. What the pricing page doesn't make obvious is how much gets billed on top, which is where most first-time buyers get a surprise on their second invoice.

Real cost: base plan ($97/$297/$497) plus usage-based fees for SMS, email, calls, and AI if you use them — the amount scales with your volume, so check current rates before you budget a fixed number.

Good deal for: anyone past a handful of users or client accounts, since it's flat-rate, not per-seat.

The Three Base Plans

Starter — $97/month. CRM, funnel builder, email marketing, SMS, calendars, social scheduling, reputation management, and a unified inbox. Agencies are capped at 3 sub-accounts (your own account plus two clients), which makes this plan a fit for solo operators or very small agencies only.

Unlimited (Pro) — $297/month. Unlimited sub-accounts and API access. This is the realistic floor for any agency managing more than a couple of client accounts.

Agency SaaS Pro — $497/month. Everything in Unlimited, plus automated SaaS billing through Stripe and a built-in configurator for reselling GoHighLevel as your own branded product.

Annual billing drops the effective monthly cost to roughly $80, $247, and $414 — about a 17% discount for committing upfront. (We cover whether that commitment makes sense in a separate breakdown, since the answer depends on how far along your setup already is.) Prices confirmed against GoHighLevel's official pricing page as of August 2026 — GoHighLevel updates pricing and usage terms fairly often, so treat these as current-best-effort, not permanent.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Starter ($97): best for solo marketers, freelancers, and anyone still testing whether GoHighLevel fits their workflow at all. The 3 sub-account cap will feel tight the moment you take on a second real client.

Unlimited ($297): the realistic starting point for most small agencies — unlimited sub-accounts and API access remove the two limits that actually bite. If you're managing multiple client accounts, this is the plan to look at first.

Agency Pro ($497): only worth it once you're actively reselling GoHighLevel under your own brand and need the built-in SaaS billing. Buying this tier before you have that use case just pays for automation you're not using yet.

Our starting recommendation for most small agencies: Unlimited ($297/month).

What's Not Included in the Sticker Price

This is the part that catches people off guard: SMS, voice calls, email sending, and AI usage are billed separately as usage-based fees on top of the base plan. GoHighLevel discloses that these charges apply and scale with volume, but doesn't publish a single flat number — because there isn't one; it depends on how many messages and calls you actually send. We're not going to guess a dollar figure here, because it would be more misleading than useful. What we can tell you: don't assume your monthly bill equals the base plan price, and check the current usage rates before you commit to a plan if your business sends high volumes of SMS or calls.

If your use case is genuinely light on outbound messaging and calls, this barely matters. If you're running an agency sending client campaigns at volume, budget for the base plan plus a real second line item for usage — and note that on Unlimited and Pro plans, agencies can rebill these usage costs to clients, often with a markup.

The AI Add-On Layer

GoHighLevel's AI features are priced separately from the base plan, in three structures: Pay-Per-Use (no monthly fee, billed by token/action cost — the lowest-commitment option), AI Employee Growth ($50/month per location, generous usage limits), and AI Employee Unlimited ($97/month per location, effectively unlimited usage on most AI products under fair-use terms). As of August 2026, GoHighLevel is also running a limited-time "Summer of AI" promotion making Ask AI and AI Studio free through September 1, 2026 — worth using while it lasts, but don't plan your long-term budget around a promotional rate. Who Should NOT Buy GoHighLevel

Skip it if you just want simple email marketing or a basic booking calendar — GoHighLevel is a 20-plus tool platform, and you'll be paying for and navigating around a lot of surface area you'll never touch. Skip it if you can't commit real setup time in the first 60–90 days; the platform has a genuine learning curve, and an unconfigured GoHighLevel account is worse than a simpler tool you actually use. And skip it if your business sends very high SMS/call volume on a tight margin without first getting a real usage-cost quote — the flat base price can hide a usage bill large enough to change the math.

The Verdict

The flat-rate structure genuinely is a good deal once you're past a handful of users or client accounts — that part of the pricing story is accurate. Where people get burned isn't the base plan, it's forgetting that usage fees and AI add-ons are separate, volume-dependent line items rather than a fixed extra. Check GoHighLevel's current usage pricing against your own SMS/email/call volume before you commit, and you won't be surprised by the invoice.
