GoHighLevel Pricing Explained (2026): What You'll Actually Pay

Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

GoHighLevel's pricing page shows three numbers: $97, $297, $497. Those are real, and they're flat-rate regardless of team size — genuinely one of the platform's best features. What the pricing page doesn't make obvious is how much gets billed on top, which is where most first-time buyers get a surprise on their second invoice.

Real cost: base plan ($97/$297/$497) plus roughly 20–30% for usage fees and AI add-ons if you use them.

Good deal for: anyone past a handful of users or client accounts, since it's flat-rate, not per-seat.

The Three Base Plans

Starter — $97/month. CRM, funnel builder, email marketing, SMS, calendars, social scheduling, reputation management, and a unified inbox. Agencies are capped at 3 sub-accounts (your own account plus two clients), which makes this plan a fit for solo operators or very small agencies only.

Unlimited (Pro) — $297/month. Unlimited sub-accounts and API access. This is the realistic floor for any agency managing more than a couple of client accounts.

Agency SaaS Pro — $497/month. Everything in Unlimited, plus automated SaaS billing through Stripe and a built-in configurator for reselling GoHighLevel as your own branded product.

Annual billing drops the effective monthly cost to roughly $80, $247, and $414 — about a 17% discount for committing upfront. (We cover whether that commitment makes sense in a separate breakdown, since the answer depends on how far along your setup already is.)

What's Not Included in the Sticker Price

This is the part that catches people off guard: SMS, voice calls, and email sending are billed separately as usage fees, typically adding $20 to $150 a month depending on volume. None of this is hidden exactly — it's disclosed in the pricing terms — but it rarely factors into anyone's mental math when they compare the $97 plan to a competitor's all-inclusive pricing.

If your use case is genuinely light on outbound messaging and calls, this barely matters. If you're running an agency sending client campaigns at volume, budget for the base plan plus a real second number for usage.

The AI Add-On Layer

On top of the base plan and usage fees, GoHighLevel's AI Employee features (Conversation AI, Voice AI, and related agents) are a separate add-on: the Growth tier runs $50/month, Unlimited $97/month, each with its own usage allocation. Eligible accounts get a 30-day free trial of the AI agents — but the trial converts to pay-per-use billing automatically unless you cancel, so it's worth putting a reminder on your calendar if you're just testing it. The Verdict

The flat-rate structure genuinely is a good deal once you're past a handful of users or client accounts — that part of the pricing story is accurate. Where people get burned isn't the base plan, it's forgetting to budget for usage fees and AI add-ons as separate line items from day one. Price your first three months assuming the base plan plus roughly 20–30% on top for usage, and you won't be surprised by the invoice.
