About SaaS Verdict

Last updated August 2026

SaaS Verdict reviews CRM, marketing automation, and AI tools for agencies, marketers, and small teams. We started it because most "best software" content is written to sell you something, not to tell you whether it actually fits your business.

What we do differently

Every review here is built around one question: who is this tool actually for, and who should skip it? That means we spend as much time on where a product falls short — pricing traps, support response times, learning curves — as we do on its strengths. If a tool isn't right for your situation, we'll say so, even when we'd earn a commission if you signed up anyway.

How we research

Reviews are built from a mix of official pricing and product pages, third-party review platforms (Capterra, G2, Trustpilot), and public user discussion (Reddit, community forums) to surface complaints and edge cases that vendor marketing pages leave out. Where claims are uncertain or vendor-reported, we say so rather than presenting them as fact.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links — if you sign up for a tool through one, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Full details are in our Affiliate Disclosure. Commission arrangements never determine our conclusions; they determine which tools we've prioritized reviewing first.

Get in touch

Questions, corrections, or a tool you'd like us to review? Reach out via the contact details listed on our affiliate program applications, or through the platforms we're active on as this site grows.
