Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated August 2026

SaaS Verdict participates in affiliate programs for some of the software we review. This page explains what that means in plain terms.

What this means for you

Some links on this site — usually the "Try [Product]" or "See pricing" buttons — are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up for a paid plan, we may earn a commission from the software company. This does not change the price you pay. In most cases, the price is identical whether you use our link or go directly to the company's site.

How this affects what we write

It doesn't determine our conclusions. Every review on this site includes both strengths and genuine drawbacks — pricing traps, support issues, features that don't work as advertised — regardless of whether we have an affiliate relationship with that company. If a tool isn't a good fit for a particular reader, we say so in the review itself, even on articles where we'd earn a commission from a signup.

What affiliate relationships do influence is which products we prioritize reviewing, since testing and researching software takes real time and this is how the site sustains itself.

Programs we currently participate in

As of August 2026, we have an active affiliate relationship with GoHighLevel. We have pending applications with Pipedrive and ActiveCampaign, which are not yet monetized on this site. Any product mentioned on this site that we don't have an affiliate relationship with is linked directly, with no commission to us.
