GoHighLevel's Voice AI Sales Workshop: Is the Free Training Worth Your Time?

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

GoHighLevel runs a live 2-day workshop on building a Voice AI sales system, led by Frank Kern (co-founder of oJoy.ai) and HighLevel's Paulson Thomas. It's marketed as free — and the training content genuinely is — but it's worth understanding exactly what "free" means here before you block out two days.

Attend if: you already have inbound call volume and want a working Voice AI system by the end of two days.

Skip if: you're pre-revenue or don't yet have a call-handling problem to automate.

What's Actually Taught

This isn't a passive webinar. The format is hands-on: identifying your ideal customer, building outreach campaigns, writing the AI prompts that qualify a lead over the phone, constructing the Voice AI workflow inside GoHighLevel step by step, and converting the resulting conversations into booked appointments. You leave with a working system, not just slides — participants get a system "snapshot" and follow-along materials rather than a recap deck.

What "Free" Actually Requires

The workshop content costs nothing. Attending it in a way that lets you build along, though, requires a GoHighLevel account — new users get a 14-day free trial, and billing starts automatically afterward unless you cancel. In other words: you're not paying for the workshop, but the workshop is structured to walk you straight into a trial that converts to a paid subscription if you don't actively opt out.

That's a reasonable trade if you were already GoHighLevel-curious. It's a less reasonable one if you're attending purely for the Voice AI education and have no intention of using the platform — you'll want a calendar reminder before day 14.

Who This Is Actually For

The target audience is explicit in how it's marketed: service businesses, agencies, and entrepreneurs who are losing leads to voicemail and want a system running in real time rather than researched in theory. If that's your situation — you have inbound call volume and no good way to capture it after hours — the hands-on format is genuinely more useful than reading a review article like this one, because you'll leave with an actual configured workflow.

If you're earlier-stage and don't yet have call volume to justify automating, the two days are better spent elsewhere. The workshop teaches you to scale a lead-qualification problem you need to already have.

A Word on the Claims

Frank Kern's own white-label business (RainMakerAI) is stated on the official workshop page as generating roughly $500K ARR running entirely on HighLevel — a real, sourced case study, but his, not a projection of your results. The workshop page itself carries the standard disclaimer that results vary and depend on effort, experience, and market conditions, and discloses that guest instructors are HighLevel affiliates who may earn commission from the workshop. Treat the $500K figure as evidence the approach can work at scale, not as an expectation to hold yourself to.

The Verdict

Worth attending if you already have inbound call volume and want a working Voice AI system by the end of two days rather than a reading list. Skip it if you're pre-revenue or don't yet have a call-handling problem to solve — and either way, put a reminder on day 13 of your trial so the "free" workshop doesn't quietly become a paid subscription you didn't mean to keep.
